Local help, fast.

Buzzby connects people with local service businesses. Tell us what you need, nearby providers send you offers, and you choose who to book.

What Buzzby does

Buzzby is a local services app. Customers post a request by typing, speaking or sending a photo. Local businesses that offer that service see the request and reply with an offer. The customer compares offers, confirms one, and can follow the job through to completion and leave a review.

Businesses use Buzzby to find new customers nearby, respond to requests, and manage their bookings.

Buzzby is operated by Lechtig LLC, 953 50th St, Brooklyn, NY 11219.

SMS messaging program

How you opt in

You opt in to Buzzby text messages when you create an account or post a request in the Buzzby app and check the box agreeing to receive SMS updates at the mobile number you provide. Opting in is not required to use Buzzby, and consent is not a condition of any purchase.

Opt-in wording shown in the app: "I agree to receive text messages from Buzzby about my requests, bookings, orders and account. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Agreeing is not required to use Buzzby. See our Terms and Privacy Policy."

Sign up by text: You can also create a Buzzby account without the app by texting JOIN to (866) 303-4499. By texting JOIN, you agree to receive text messages from Buzzby about your requests, bookings, orders and account at that number. You will get one reply confirming your sign-up, with how to opt out and get help. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Agreeing is not required to use Buzzby, and you can use the app instead.

What we send

Messages relate only to your own account and requests: sign-in verification codes, updates when a business responds to your request, booking confirmations and changes, and job status updates. For businesses: alerts about new customer requests and booking updates. We do not send marketing messages through this program.

How often

Message frequency varies with your activity on Buzzby. Most people receive a few messages per request.

Msg & data rates may apply.

Stopping and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You will get one message confirming you have been unsubscribed. Reply HELP for help, or contact us by email or phone below.
